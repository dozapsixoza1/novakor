# NOVACORE Technologies — Operating structure

This is a target operating model, not a claim that every role or department is currently staffed. Assignments should be made only when people have actually joined and accepted responsibility.

## Leadership
- Founder / CEO — company direction, priorities, product vision, and final decisions.
- CTO — architecture, engineering standards, security, and technical delivery.
- COO — operations, planning, process quality, and delivery coordination.
- CMO / Growth Lead — positioning, marketing, communications, and partnerships.
- Finance & Administration Lead — budgeting, records, billing operations, and administration.

One person may temporarily cover multiple functions. Keep decision ownership explicit and avoid creating titles without corresponding responsibilities.

## Core divisions
1. **Development** — websites, web applications, APIs, dashboards, and custom software.
2. **Automation** — bots, integrations, internal tools, and workflow automation.
3. **Products** — discovery, product management, engineering, releases, and product support for owned products.
4. **Design** — research, UX, UI, brand systems, and prototyping.
5. **Infrastructure** — hosting, deployment, CI/CD, observability, backups, and reliability.
6. **Labs** — prototypes, technical research, and controlled experiments.
7. **Business operations** — sales, marketing, customer success, finance, and administration.
8. **Quality & Security** — testing, release checks, access control, and security review; initially shared across engineering.

## Delivery lifecycle
1. Discovery: understand the problem, users, constraints, and success criteria.
2. Scope: define deliverables, exclusions, milestones, and acceptance criteria.
3. Design: map user flows, interfaces, and system architecture.
4. Build: implement in reviewed, version-controlled changes.
5. Verify: type checks, automated tests, security checks, and manual QA.
6. Release: deploy with rollback and monitoring plans.
7. Support: document ownership, incidents, and improvement work.

## Initial staffing principle
Start as a small accountable team. Add a role only when there is sustained work for it, a clear owner, and a realistic operating budget. Use qualified adult support for legal, contractual, banking, and regulated business matters where required by local law.
