# NOVACORE Technologies — Brand foundation

## Positioning
NOVACORE Technologies is a technology initiative focused on custom software, automation, digital products, design, and dependable infrastructure.

**Primary tagline:** Building the next generation of technology.  
**Supporting line:** We don't just build software. We build technology.

## Mission
Turn ideas and operational challenges into useful, understandable, and reliable digital systems.

## Vision
Build a technology company that combines client engineering work with a portfolio of independent digital products.

## Brand character
- Precise, capable, and calm.
- Modern without chasing short-lived visual trends.
- Ambitious, but honest about current capabilities and delivery status.
- Clear language; no inflated claims, invented clients, or unsupported statistics.

## Visual identity
- Background: near-black `#080A0E`.
- Panels: graphite `#0E1219` and `#111722`.
- Primary text: `#F2F5F9`.
- Secondary text: `#929BAA`.
- Accent blue: `#8ABAFF`; strong accent `#4B96F5`.
- Typography: Manrope for interface and display; DM Mono for technical labels.
- Symbol: geometric, forward-stepping N mark.
- Layout: generous spacing, thin dividers, restrained blue light, strong typographic hierarchy.

## Voice
Use direct, specific language. Describe what is available today separately from what is planned. Avoid claiming global reach, customer results, certifications, or a staffed corporate structure until verified.

## Launch checklist
- Confirm legal availability of the NOVACORE name and logo in relevant jurisdictions.
- Search trademark databases and company registers before commercial use.
- Secure the final domain and matching official social handles.
- Replace the temporary contact address on the website.
- Add verified legal entity details, privacy notice, terms, and cookie notice if required.
- Confirm ownership and licensing of all fonts, assets, and third-party services.
